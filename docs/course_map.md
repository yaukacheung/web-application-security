## Assessment Alignment

- Weeks 1-8 support the midterm multiple choice, short answer, and practical exam.
- Weeks 9-16 support the final exam and capstone project.
- Every lab requires scope, baseline behavior, evidence, impact, remediation, and reflection.
- The capstone combines methodology, evidence quality, risk communication, and remediation planning.
