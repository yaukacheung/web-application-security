# Week 15 Student Lab

## Capstone Testing Workshop

## Target

Assigned capstone target

## What You Will Practice

You will turn validated findings into a professional report. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Peer-review scope
2. Improve finding evidence
3. Write severity rationale
4. Draft the executive summary

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
