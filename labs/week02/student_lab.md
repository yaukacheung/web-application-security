# Week 2 Student Lab

## Application Mapping and Attack Surface Inventory

## Target

OWASP Juice Shop

## What You Will Practice

You will map visible application behavior and identify trust boundaries. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Build a route inventory
2. Map roles and assets
3. Create a trust boundary sketch
4. Prioritize three likely risks

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
