# Week 8 Student Lab

## Midterm Practical Exam

## Target

Assigned local target

## What You Will Practice

You will perform a concise scoped assessment under exam conditions. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Map the assigned target
2. Select one valid issue
3. Collect minimal evidence
4. Submit a practical finding

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
