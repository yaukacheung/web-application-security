# Week 1 Student Lab

## Environment Validation and Baseline Web Observation

## Target

Local lab stack

## What You Will Practice

You will establish scope, observe HTTP traffic, and document baseline behavior. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Verify all local targets
2. Inspect one request and response
3. Write a scope statement
4. Submit baseline evidence

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
