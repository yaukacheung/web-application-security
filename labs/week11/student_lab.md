# Week 11 Student Lab

## SSRF Lab and Service Boundary Analysis

## Target

WebGoat and Juice Shop

## What You Will Practice

You will trace server-side requests and dangerous object handling assumptions. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Identify server-side fetch behavior
2. Map reachable destinations
3. Analyze deserialization risk
4. Recommend service-boundary controls

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
