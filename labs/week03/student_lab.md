# Week 3 Student Lab

## SQL Injection in Controlled Lab Targets

## Target

DVWA and WebGoat

## What You Will Practice

You will validate injection risk and explain parameterized query defenses. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Identify injectable inputs
2. Compare normal and altered requests
3. Capture database-impact evidence
4. Recommend a query fix

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
