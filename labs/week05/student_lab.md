# Week 5 Student Lab

## Authentication Testing and Credential Attack Controls

## Target

WebGoat and Juice Shop

## What You Will Practice

You will evaluate authentication flows without using real credentials. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Map login and recovery flows
2. Test rate-limit behavior safely
3. Review error-message differences
4. Recommend authentication hardening

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
