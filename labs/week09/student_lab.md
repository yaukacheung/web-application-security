# Week 9 Student Lab

## CSRF Testing and SameSite Defenses

## Target

WebGoat and Mutillidae II

## What You Will Practice

You will analyze state-changing requests and browser-side defenses. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Identify state-changing requests
2. Check token behavior
3. Evaluate SameSite attributes
4. Recommend browser and server controls

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
