## What You Will Practice

You will connect findings to lifecycle controls and dependency risk. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

- Scope statement
- Target and role used

- Reproduction or observation steps

- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty
