# Week 7 Student Lab

## Authorization Testing and Object-Level Access Control

## Target

Juice Shop and WebGoat

## What You Will Practice

You will test object-level authorization with controlled role changes. Work only inside the assigned local lab environment.

## Tasks

1. Create two test roles
2. Identify object identifiers
3. Attempt scoped cross-user access
4. Document expected and actual results

## Evidence To Submit

- Scope statement
- Target and role used
- Baseline behavior
- Reproduction or observation steps
- Minimal screenshots or request/response excerpts
- Impact explanation
- Remediation recommendation
- Limitation or uncertainty

## Safety Reminder

Do not test outside the assigned local target. Do not include real passwords, complete session tokens, or unrelated personal data in your submission.
